# 12.3 — The Sky Ruins

Dungeon 10, unlocked by finishing **The Shadow World**. Plus two races
(Aarakocra, Goliath), two classes (Plague Doctor, Artificer) and a new
difficulty, **Impossible**. Same content on iPhone, Mac, Vision Pro, web
and Android.

---

## The story

> Long before Malachar, a kingdom lived above the clouds. Its gardeners
> grew plants that could heal any wound, until one of them, **Queen
> Verdana**, bred a flower that could not die. It spread. It strangled the
> towers, poisoned the wells, and tore the whole kingdom out of the earth,
> roots and all. Now the ruins float above the dungeons, and the garden is
> still growing. It wants new soil. It wants you.

**Intro (entering):** *"The stairs end in open sky. Below you, clouds. Ahead,
broken towers wrapped in vines thicker than your arm. Something sweet is in
the air. It burns a little when you breathe."*

**Victory:** *"Verdana's last root splits. The towers groan, and for the first
time in a thousand years the ruins begin, slowly, to sink back toward the
earth. Somewhere below, a single seed falls with them."*

## The three mechanics

### 1. Deadly poison (stacks)
- Many Sky Ruins enemies poison on hit. **Poison stacks**: each new dose
  adds **+2 damage per turn**, up to **5 stacks (10/turn)**, lasting **4
  turns** (refreshed by each new dose).
- **Antidote** (new item, shop 45g, also dropped): clears all poison.
  Health potions don't.
- Poison ticks at the start of your turn and can't take you below 1 HP
  outside a boss fight (bosses can finish you).
- Spoken: *"Poisoned, 3 stacks, 6 damage a turn."*

### 2. Killer plants (room hazards)
Some rooms have a plant in them before or instead of an enemy:
- 🌿 **Strangling Ivy:** grabs you; your next action is spent breaking free
  (Goliath and Artificer break free instantly).
- 🍄 **Spore Pod:** bursts into a cloud: 1 poison stack unless you
  "hold your breath" (a quick choice: Search or Move on).
- 🌹 **Thornwall:** blocks the way. Cut through (take 8 damage) or go around
  (lose a room's loot).
- 🌸 **Siren Bloom (rare):** a sweet smell; heals 15 HP… and adds 2 poison
  stacks. Can be ignored.

### 3. Wind
- 1 room in 6 has a **gust**: *"The wind tears at you!"* Choose **Brace**
  (lose 5 HP) or **Let it carry you** (skip ahead one room, maybe past loot,
  maybe past danger).
- On rare **Edge** rooms a fall drops you **one floor down** (lose 10% HP).
  Aarakocra glide: no damage, and they pick which way to land.

### Boss (floor 10): Queen Verdana, the Undying Bloom 🌹
- **HP 260**, ATK 18–28, gold 60–90.
- **Phase 1 (100–60%):** *"Welcome to my garden."* Poisoning vines (1
  stack per hit).
- **Phase 2 (60–25%):** *"You'll make such lovely soil."* Summons a
  **Thornwall**: her damage is halved until you cut it (one attack).
- **Phase 3 (25–0%):** *"I cannot die. I am the garden."* She **regrows
  20 HP a turn from her roots** unless you've **burned the roots**: fire
  skills, a fire weapon, or the new **Root Torch** item (dropped by Hollow
  Gardeners, sold at the last shop). Players are warned before phase 3.
- **Mini-boss (floor 5): The Groundskeeper** 🪓: HP 140, ATK 15–23, a
  giant pruning hook; every 3rd turn a sweep that bleeds 4/turn for 3 turns.

## New races

### 🪶 Aarakocra (bird-folk)
- **Glide:** no fall damage; choose where to land after a fall or gust.
- **Keen Eyes:** +15% chance to spot traps and hidden rooms.
- **Hollow Bones:** −10% max HP, +8% dodge.
- Description: "Bird-folk of the high peaks. Light, quick and sharp-eyed."

### 🗿 Goliath (giant-folk)
- **Stone's Endurance:** once per fight, a heavy hit is reduced by half.
- **Mountain Born:** immune to gusts and Strangling Ivy.
- **Powerful Build:** +15% max HP, −5% dodge.
- Description: "Giant-folk of the mountains. Slow to fall, hard to move."

## New classes

### 🩺 Plague Doctor ("The Cure Is Worse")
- HP 95, ATK 7–14, DEF 2.
- **Passive: Bird Mask:** poison does 50% less to you; immune to Spore Pods.
- Skills (unlocking with level like other classes):
  1. **Toxic Flask** (8 SP): poison the enemy, 2 stacks (3 dmg each, stacking up to 5).
  2. **Remedy** (10 SP): clear your poison and heal 20 HP.
  3. **Leeches** (12 SP): drain 15 HP from the enemy.
  4. **Miasma** (16 SP): poison every enemy (ambushes), 3 stacks.
  5. **Black Death** (22 SP, once per fight): the enemy's poison stacks
     explode for 12 damage each.
- Trait (Class Traits on): poisoned enemies deal 15% less damage.

### ⚙️ Artificer ("Built, Not Born")
- HP 100, ATK 6–12, DEF 3.
- **Passive: Tinkerer:** +1 gadget at the start of each floor; scrap from kills.
- Skills:
  1. **Deploy Turret** (10 SP): a turret that shoots for 5–8 every turn for 3 turns.
  2. **Shock Gauntlet** (8 SP): 12–18 damage, 30% stun.
  3. **Repair Kit** (10 SP): heal 18 HP and fix 20 weapon durability.
  4. **Flamethrower** (14 SP): 14–20 fire damage (burns roots and regrowth).
  5. **Overclock** (20 SP, once per fight): your next 2 attacks deal double.
- Trait: turrets last 1 turn longer; gadgets never break.

## 💀 Impossible mode (difficulty above Insane)
- **One life.** No revives, no Phoenix, no Death's Bargain.
- Enemies **+80% HP and +60% attack**; bosses get an extra phase message.
- **No shop on floors 4–6.** Potions heal **half**.
- Elites appear twice as often.
- Rewards: **double gold and XP**, the title **"The Impossible"** for
  clearing any dungeon, and **"Beyond Impossible"** for clearing the Sky
  Ruins on it.
- Its own **Impossible leaderboard** (Game Center and online).
- Warning at creation: *"Impossible. One life. Everything is stronger.
  Most players will not finish a single floor. Are you sure?"*
