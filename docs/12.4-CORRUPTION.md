# 12.4 — The Corruption Update

Damage types, enemies that are weak or strong to them, corruption you can
weaponize (and that creeps into you), enchantments, the Enchanter's Sanctum
and Rubies. Web first (js/38-…), then DungeonCore and the apps, like 12.3.

The game never says "super effective". Players learn by the numbers: a hit
that usually does 300 does 900. VoiceOver reads the same number. The
Bestiary remembers what you've learned.

---

## 1. Damage types

| Type | Icon | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | ⚔️ | Basic attacks, heavy attacks, most warrior skills |
| Fire | 🔥 | fire, flame, burn, ember, inferno, blaze, ash, magma |
| Ice | ❄️ | ice, frost, freeze, cold, glacier, snow, rime |
| Storm | ⚡ | storm, lightning, thunder, shock, tempest, wind, tornado |
| Poison | ☠️ | poison, venom, toxic, plague, blight, acid, spore |
| Holy | ✨ | holy, divine, smite, radiant, sacred, light, prayer, judgment |
| Arcane | 🔮 | arcane, mana, spell, rune, time, astral (nothing resists it much) |
| Corruption | 🌑 | corrupt, void, shadow, curse, hex, abyss, soul, dark, blood magic |

- Every skill gets one type, decided from its name and description by those
  words (first match in the order Corruption, Holy, Fire, Ice, Storm,
  Poison, Arcane, else Physical). A small override table fixes the few that
  read wrong (e.g. "Blood Rage" is Physical, "Soulfire" is Fire).
- Basic and heavy attacks are Physical, unless the weapon's enchantment
  changes them (Emberbrand makes them Fire, and so on).
- The combat log shows the type with the number: "🔥 Fireball hits for 942."

## 2. Weak and strong

| Affinity | Damage taken |
|---|---|
| Very weak | ×2.5 |
| Weak | ×1.75 |
| Normal | ×1 |
| Resists | ×0.5 |
| Immune | ×0 (at least 1) |
| Absorbs | heals by half the hit (only void things vs Corruption) |

Each enemy's affinities come from rules, so all of them get some (including
community enemies), first match wins per type:

1. **Bosses**: a hand-written table (below).
2. **Name words**:
   - frost / ice / snow / glacier / rime → weak Fire, immune Ice
   - flame / fire / ember / ash / magma / infernal / cinder → weak Ice, immune Fire
   - storm / thunder / lightning / sky / wind / gale → weak Ice, resists Storm
   - plant / vine / thorn / bloom / root / fig / moss / spore → weak Fire, resists Poison
   - void / shadow / abyss / umbral / null → weak Holy, absorbs Corruption
   - angel / saint / priest / seraph / holy / paladin → very weak Corruption, resists Holy
   - golem / construct / armored / stone / iron → resists Physical, weak Storm
3. **Creature type** (Bestiary enemyType):
   - undead → weak Holy, immune Poison, resists Ice
   - spirit → weak Holy, resists Physical
   - beast → weak Fire, weak Poison
   - demon → weak Holy, resists Fire
   - elemental → by its element (as in 2)
4. **The dungeon**, for anything still unset:
   - The Ashbound Wastes / The Infernal Descent → resists Fire, weak Ice
   - Frosthold Abyss → resists Ice, weak Fire
   - The Sunken Archives → weak Storm
   - The Shadow World / The Abyssal Deep → weak Holy
   - The Sky Ruins → weak Fire (plants), resists Poison

Bosses (examples; every boss gets a line):

| Boss | Weak | Resists |
|---|---|---|
| Malachar | Holy (×2.5) | Corruption, Poison |
| Queen Verdana | Fire | Poison, absorbs nothing |
| The Time Eater | Arcane | everything else ×0.75 |
| The Void Reaper | Holy | absorbs Corruption |

Balance guard: no enemy is immune to more than one type, and Physical is
never worse than ×0.5, so every class can still win every fight.

**The Bestiary** shows "Weak to 🔥 · Resists ❄️" for an enemy, but only the
types you've actually hit it with (saved per player, like kills).

## 3. Corruption (on enemies)

Corruption hits build a meter on the enemy, 0 to 100:

- A Corruption skill adds 15 to 35 (by its SP cost), the Corrupting
  enchantment adds 10 per hit.
- Very weak enemies gain it twice as fast. Void things (absorb) never gain it.

| Meter | Stage | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | 🌑 Unsettled | 20% of its attacks miss on their own |
| 50 | 🌀 Maddened | each turn: 25% it hits itself (its own attack), 15% it just screams (loses the turn) |
| 100 | 💀 Consumed | dies at once: "💀 The corruption consumes the Frost Wraith — 3,214 corrupted damage." (its HP left) |

Bosses can't be Consumed: at 100 they take 15% of their max HP, go
Maddened for 3 turns, and the meter starts again from 0.

The meter shows on the enemy bar and is read with it ("Corruption 60,
Maddened").

## 4. Corruption (on you)

Each Corruption hit you deal adds 5 to your own meter (0–100); it lasts the
whole run.

| Your meter | Effect |
|---|---|
| 25 | +10% damage |
| 50 | +20% damage, 10% of turns you lose control (hit yourself for 5% max HP) |
| 75 | +30% damage, 20% lose control |
| 100 | the corruption breaks out: you take 25% max HP and drop back to 50 |

**Any potion clears all of it** ("The potion burns the darkness out of you.
Your mind is clear."). So potions become a choice: stay sane, or ride the
power a little longer. The Clearmind armor enchantment halves how fast it
builds.

## 5. Enchantments

Weapons and armor get enchantment slots by rarity: common 1, rare 2,
legendary 3 (items without a rarity: by attack/defense tier).

**Weapon**

| Enchantment | Effect | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Staggering Blow | 20%: the enemy reels, loses its next attack and takes 25% extra impact damage | 800 gold |
| Emberbrand | attacks become 🔥 Fire; 25% to set it burning (3 turns, 4% of its max HP) | 1,000 gold |
| Rimebite | attacks become ❄️ Ice; 20% to slow it (skips a turn) | 1,000 gold |
| Stormcall | attacks become ⚡ Storm; 15% a bolt jumps for 50% more | 1,000 gold |
| Venomkiss | attacks become ☠️ Poison; each hit stacks 2 poison damage a turn (max 5) | 1,000 gold |
| Hallowed | attacks become ✨ Holy | 6 diamonds |
| Bloodthirst | heals you for 12% of the damage you deal | 8 diamonds |
| Sundering | each hit makes it take 6% more damage, up to 30% | 10 diamonds |
| Twinstrike | 15% to hit twice | 12 diamonds |
| Executioner | ×2.5 damage to enemies below 20% HP | 5 Rubies |
| Corrupting | attacks become 🌑 Corruption and add 10 to its meter | 8 Rubies |

**Armor**

| Enchantment | Effect | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Briarguard | enemies who hit you take 20% of it back | 900 gold |
| Warding (Fire / Ice / Storm / Poison / Corruption) | that type hurts you 50% less | 1,200 gold |
| Second Wind | once a fight, heal 30% when you drop below 25% | 10 diamonds |
| Clearmind | your corruption builds half as fast | 4 Rubies |

- One enchantment of each name per item.
- Removing one costs 200 gold (nothing back), so trying things is cheap.
- **Enchanted Tomes** drop sometimes (elites 5%, bosses 20%): a free
  enchantment of a random kind, applied at the Sanctum.

## 6. The Enchanter's Sanctum

A new place on the main menu (Modes tab in the apps, next to the
Blacksmith):

1. Pick a weapon or armor from your items (equipped ones first).
2. See its slots and what's in them, then the enchantments you can add,
   each with its cost and effect.
3. Pay with gold, diamonds or Rubies (shown at the top), and it's done.

The Enchanter is Morwen, who has seen what corruption does to people. A
few lines of hers at the door and when you buy ("Fire remembers being a
star. Be gentle with it."). Calm music (the codex track until it has its
own).

## 7. Rubies ♦️

A new currency that can't be bought, only earned on hard difficulties:

| From | Rubies |
|---|---|
| Elite, Forsaken or harder | 15% chance of 1 |
| Boss on Forsaken | 1 |
| Boss on Nightmare | 2 |
| Boss on Insane | 3 |
| Boss on Impossible | 5 |
| Clearing a dungeon on Impossible (first time each dungeon) | 10 |

Shown next to gold, diamonds and Mystery Coins. Saved and synced like the
others ("dd_rubies").

## 9. Testing

- Bot tests (like the Sky Ruins'): every class fights through each dungeon
  with random skills and enchantments; nothing may break, every class can
  still beat every boss.
- Unit tests: the type of every skill, every enemy's affinities, the
  corruption stages and the boss rule, potions clearing your corruption,
  enchantment costs and slots, Ruby drops per difficulty.
