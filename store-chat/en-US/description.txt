Dungeon Descent Chat is the official community app for Dungeon Descent, the dungeon crawler you can play completely by ear. Talk with other players, get help, find co-op partners and hear the news first, in an app built for VoiceOver and screen reader users from the ground up. Made by a blind developer.

BUILT FOR VOICEOVER
• Every channel, message and button is labeled and announced.
• New messages are read aloud while you're in a channel.
• Simple layout: channels on one screen, the conversation on the next.

CHANNELS FOR EVERYTHING
• Announcements: news and updates straight from the developer.
• General, Help and Strategy: talk about the game and get help.
• Bug Reports and Ideas: tell us what's broken and what you'd like next.
• Hall of Legends: show off your best runs.
• Co-op Rooms: find players for co-op dungeons and raids.
• Community Enemies and Community Dungeons: share what you've made.

PRIVATE MESSAGES
• Message other players one on one.
• Turn private messages off any time.

YOUR NOTIFICATIONS, YOUR SOUNDS
• Choose which channels notify you.
• Pick notification sounds from the game itself: achievement chimes, unlock sounds and more.

SAFE BY DESIGN
• Sign in with the same account you use in Dungeon Descent (Apple, Google or email).
• A word filter, and Report and Block on every message.
• Moderators review every report within 24 hours.
• Delete your account any time from Settings.

No ads. No purchases. Just the Dungeon Descent community.
