Dungeon Descent Chat is the community app for the game Dungeon Descent (same developer, same accounts). Sign in with the demo account below. Channels are on the first screen; open one to read and post. Every message from another player has Report and Block (swipe actions or the VoiceOver Actions rotor). Settings has Notifications, Blocked Players and Delete Account. Moderators review reports within 24 hours; banned players can't post. The app is designed for VoiceOver users, so every control has a spoken label.
