Descend into a dark fantasy dungeon you can play completely by ear. Dungeon Descent is a turn-based dungeon crawler RPG built from the ground up for VoiceOver and screen reader players, and fun for everyone. Made by a blind developer.

FULLY ACCESSIBLE
• Every button, room, enemy, attack and piece of loot is announced.
• Works with VoiceOver, voice commands, a keyboard and buttons.
• A step-by-step tutorial teaches everything.

FORGE YOUR LEGEND
• 24 classes, from Warrior and Paladin to Necromancer and Bard.
• 15 races and 11 professions, each with its own strengths.
• Choose your dungeon and your difficulty, from forgiving to one life only.

FIGHT, LOOT, SURVIVE
• Turn-based combat: attack, heavy strike, defend, potions, spells and class skills.
• Weapons, armor, runes, scrolls, a blacksmith and an arcane shop.
• Rooms full of traps, treasure, curses and bosses.

ENDLESS THINGS TO DO
• Endless Descent, The Spire, Void Mode, Boss Rush and more modes.
• Quests, daily challenges and seasonal events all year, including Día de los Muertos, Lunar New Year, the Eclipse and Monster Migration, plus a bestiary of hundreds of creatures.
• 100 Game Center achievements.

PLAY WITH OTHERS (OPTIONAL)
• Global chat, community-made enemies, leaderboards and tournaments.
• Every message and community enemy can be reported or blocked.

YOUR SAVE IS SAFE
• Progress saves on your device, with optional iCloud backup.
• An optional account syncs your progress. Sign in with Apple or email, and delete your account any time in the game.

No ads. No purchases. Put on headphones and descend.
