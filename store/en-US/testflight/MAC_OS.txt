Thanks for testing Dungeon Descent on Mac!

Please try:
• Playing with the keyboard: Ctrl+Shift+letter opens screens (H menu, P characters, B bestiary, Q quests, S settings), and in combat Option+A attack, H heavy, D defend, P potion, F flee, S spells, E examine, 1–3 skills, R status. I opens the inventory and Backspace the pause menu.
• Resizing the window, VoiceOver on Mac, and signing in with Apple or email so your cloud save comes with you.
• A full run through a dungeon, plus Endless Descent.

Report problems with a screenshot from TestFlight, or in the Discord.
