Thanks for testing Dungeon Descent on Apple Vision Pro!

New in 11.9: the daily World Boss (main menu), the Weekly Ladder (Challenges), Game Center leaderboards and friend challenges, Siri and Shortcuts, the Welcome back reward, "What just happened" (two-finger double-tap in a run), and much more Portuguese.

Please try:
• Moving around the menus and a dungeon run with look and pinch, and with VoiceOver.
• Whether text is easy to read and the window is a comfortable size.
• Sound: music, effects and the dungeon ambience.
• Signing in with Apple or email so your cloud save comes with you.

Report problems with a screenshot from TestFlight, or in the Discord.
