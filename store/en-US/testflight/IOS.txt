Thanks for testing Dungeon Descent 12.0!

New, please try:
• The new main menu: tabs along the bottom (Play, Modes, Multiplayer, More, Settings). Settings, Gameplay, Classic Menu brings back the old list.
• Co-op Dungeon (Multiplayer tab): ten floors with up to three friends, on the web and the apps together. Share the 4-letter room code.
• Battle Pass: 50 tiers earned by playing, never bought, with five new quests every Monday.
• Prestige: a character at their level cap can start over for a permanent star (on the character's card).
• Cloud sync: your whole save travels between the web and the apps. Sign in on both and play on one, then open the other.
• Audio Map (in the dungeon): plays the floor as sounds around you. Best with headphones; try it in 3D Mode too.
• Portuguese: the whole game should now be in Portuguese. Developer Tools, Missing Portuguese records anything still in English.
• Apple Watch: a new home screen with World Boss strikes, the Battle Pass, the daily reward and Quick Fight (turn the Digital Crown to pick a move). Wrist Alerts tap you for low HP, elites, the hunter and your co-op turn while you play on the iPhone with the watch app open. Siri on the watch, and new complications: Endless Descent, Daily Streak and the Battle Pass.
