Thanks for testing Dungeon Descent 12.2.1!

New in this build — please try:
• Fixed: 25 skills could be cast over and over at 0 SP with an Apocalypse weapon to stack endless dodges, attack, defense, free casts or skill damage (Wind Step, Divine Shield, Shadow Form, Bless, The Lich King, Dark Ascension, Unlimited Power, Maestro, the Masteries and more). Dodges and free casts now top up instead of stacking, and the other bonuses work once per fight. If you play one of these classes, tell us how it feels.

Found a bug? Tell us in bug-reports on Discord, or use Send Feedback in Settings: what happened, what you were doing, and your device.
