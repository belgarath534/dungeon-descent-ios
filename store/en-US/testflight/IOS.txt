Thanks for testing Dungeon Descent 12.2!

New in this build — please try:
• Guilds (Multiplayer tab, needs an account): create or join a guild, talk in Guild Chat, and strike the weekly Guild Boss (5 strikes a day). Try Members, Guild Rankings, and Manage Guild as the leader.
• Co-op and Raids for five players: up to four friends in one room.
• The Mine goes deeper: bosses at depths 75, 150, 200, 300 and 500, and every 100 from 600. Mine bosses from depth 100 drop Heartstone Shards.
• The Mountainbreaker: forge it at the Blacksmith (Buy Weapons) from 5 Heartstone Shards.
• New guns: Harpoon Gun, Flare Gun, Blunderbuss and Tesla Coil. Every gun has its own sound.
• Rare rooms: the Wishing Well and the Forgotten Music Room.
• My Enemies (Bestiary, Community, needs an account): edit or delete the enemies you made.

Found a bug? Tell us in bug-reports on Discord, or use Send Feedback in Settings: what happened, what you were doing, and your device.
