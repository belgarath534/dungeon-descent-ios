Thanks for testing Dungeon Descent 12.1.1!

New in this build — please try:
• The Magic Chamber: a rare, glowing room in any dungeon (not the Abyssal Deep or the Shadow World). A unicorn waits inside. Approach it and Starlight the Unicorn joins you as a minion; choose it in your Home under Minions. It fights beside you and heals you 3 HP every round. Finding the chamber again restores your HP and SP. Leave it in peace for 10 HP.

Found a bug? Tell us in bug-reports on Discord, or use Send Feedback in Settings: what happened, what you were doing, and your device.
