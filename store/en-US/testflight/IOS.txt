Thanks for testing Dungeon Descent 11.9!

New, please try:
• World Boss (main menu): one boss a day that every player fights together, 10 strikes each. If it falls, everyone who struck gets gold and Soul Fragments. Sign in so your damage counts for everyone.
• Weekly Ladder (Challenges, third tab): one dungeon all week, one life, a top 10 that resets every Monday.
• Challenges, "Challenge a friend": Game Center leaderboards for Endless Descent, the Weekly Ladder and The Spire, with Game Center's Challenge button.
• Endless Descent rivals: the Lock Screen shows the next player above your floor, and who you just passed.
• Apple Watch: a Daily World Boss complication for your watch face.
• Siri and Shortcuts: "Start Endless Descent", "How's my run" and "Use a potion".
• Welcome back: after an hour or more away, your first character comes back with gold and Soul Fragments.
• In a run: two-finger double-tap (Magic Tap) or the "What just happened" action reads the last few lines. Settings, Accessibility: short combat lines and warning sounds.
• My Tracks: add songs from your Apple Music library.
• Portuguese: much more of the combat and events is now in Portuguese.
• The loading screen at launch, the Music Box tutorial, and your total playtime in the pause menu.

Fixed:
• The Spire's Auto Mode with VoiceOver reads the floor you're on, not one from minutes ago.
• The Lock Screen keeps moving in Endless Descent even when you aren't signed in.
• No music starts on its own while the app isn't open.
