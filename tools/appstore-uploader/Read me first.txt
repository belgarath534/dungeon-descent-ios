Dungeon Descent App Store uploader

1. Unzip this folder on your Windows computer.
2. Copy your key file into this folder. It is the file you downloaded from Apple, named AuthKey_ followed by letters, ending in .p8. It is the same key you used for Accessible Backrooms.
3. Double click "Fill in App Store page.bat".
4. When it asks, paste your Issuer ID and press Enter.
5. If it asks for your name, phone and email, type them. Only Apple reviewers see them.
6. Wait until it says ALL DONE, then refresh App Store Connect.

It fills in: description, subtitle, keywords, promotional text, App Review notes, category, the newest build and the iPhone and iPad screenshots.
It does not change the app name, or your support, marketing and privacy links if they are already filled in.
Your key stays on your computer. It is only sent to Apple, to sign in.
You can run it again safely: it replaces the screenshots instead of adding copies.
